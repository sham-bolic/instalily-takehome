# **Objective Background**

Instalily is deploying **Lily AI Agents** to automate **Lead Generation & Outreach:** automatically gather prospect information, enhance their profiles with relevant details, and craft personalized outreach messages—all while giving our sales team the flexibility to review, edit, or send with just one click.

**Your Project/ Goal:** Build a working prototype of AI assistants that can generate and manage sales leads, using DuPont Tedlar for their Graphics & Signage team as your test case.

Automate **data extraction, enrichment, and (ideally) a personalized outreach note to the lead.** Ensure **scalability and automation. Identify** and **qualify** credible leads for **DuPont Tedlar**’s sales team by researching key industry events, trade associations, and relevant gatherings where DuPont’s **Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)** will be present. Present findings in a dashboard layout with **event**, **company**, **rationale for qualification**, (and **personalized outreach message).**

# **Scope of Work**

- **Event & Association Research –** Find relevant trade associations, industry events, and bodies where target customers are likely to attend (based on public information).
- **Company Sourcing & Prioritization –** Identify companies attending these events and prioritize them by approximate revenue or size using public data.
- **Stakeholder Identification –** Locate key decision-makers (e.g., VPs, Directors) and, where possible, include LinkedIn Sales Navigator profile links.
- **Deliverable Format –** Present findings in a dashboard layout with event, company, rationale for qualification, and personalized outreach message.
- **Integrations:** Provisions (no need for the actual account but think through how this integration would be added into your code) for integrating a LinkedIn Sales Navigator API, or Clay API, or other resources to surface individuals and their contact information.

*Nice to have*: **Outreach –** Ideally, this also would draft a brief outreach note tailored to decision-makers; ensure **data validation and error handling**.
