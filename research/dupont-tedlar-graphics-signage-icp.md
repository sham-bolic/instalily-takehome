# DuPont Tedlar Graphics and Signage ICP notes

Retrieved: 2026-08-17

## First-party findings

DuPont describes Tedlar Clear Protection as a transparent PVF surface laminate for graphics. The product is positioned to protect indoor and outdoor graphics from fading, corrosion, harsh UV exposure, graffiti, dirt, moisture, and chemicals while reducing cleaning, maintenance, and replacement costs.[1][2][3]

DuPont names these graphics and signage applications:

- Signs and business logos
- Outdoor billboards and signage
- Building murals
- Traffic and utility box wraps
- Vehicle and fleet graphics
- Architectural exterior graphics

DuPont also emphasizes long outdoor life, color and mechanical-property retention, cleanability, and resistance to yellowing, cracking, mold, and mildew in demanding environments.[1][2][3]

## ICP implications

The strongest target companies are manufacturers and converters whose products can incorporate a clear protective overlaminate, especially producers of graphic films, signage materials, vehicle-wrap materials, and durable finished graphics. Strong need signals include outdoor exposure, expensive cleaning or replacement, graffiti risk, harsh UV or moisture, and a premium durability product strategy.

These target-company conclusions are ICP interpretations of DuPont's product and application information. They are not direct statements by DuPont about its customer list.
